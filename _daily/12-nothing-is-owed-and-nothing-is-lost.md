# Nothing is owed, and nothing is lost.

**Why it is true.** There is no judge at the top and no ledger kept there — so nothing is owed, by me or to me, and there was never a charge to be forgiven. And every harm counts, totally and forever, and never needed anyone to suffer in order to be kept. Say both halves, always: the first alone reads as amnesty; the second alone reads as a sentence. What the harmed person needed was never the suffering. It was that the harm count, and it already does.

**Fence.** Nothing owed is not nothing done. What I did is mine, and I carry it. Responsibility stays; consequence stays; repair stays. What goes is only the idea that the universe is owed my suffering.

**Carry.** *Am I repairing, or paying?*

— *What CT Concludes*, "What matters"; *Nobody Keeps Score* §§5–6; *Habits of Mind* §2, habit 7.
