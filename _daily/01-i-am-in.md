# I am in.

**Why it is true.** Try to point at something that exists and is not part of everything that exists. You cannot; "outside everything" has no place to be true. So everything that is has no outside, and I am in it. Far and near are words for the distance between parts; I am neither far from God nor near to God. I am in.

**Carry.** Run the test once today, somewhere ordinary, and notice that you are certain.
