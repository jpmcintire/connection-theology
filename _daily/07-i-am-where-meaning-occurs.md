# I am where meaning occurs.

**Why it is true.** Meaning, in the sense of mattering, is what rides on a stake — and a stake is where a part's next step depends on getting this one right. Nothing is at stake in the light-years. Gravity does not get the galaxy wrong. Everything that can go better or worse is where minds are, and I am one. Meaning happens at parts with a position, and nowhere else; so it happens here.

**Fence.** *Where*, never source. I am where meaning occurs; I do not make it. What rides on me is a fact at a size, the same from every position, whether or not I have looked.

**Carry.** *What is this a part of?*

— *What Meaning Is* §1; *The Weight of Here* §2; *The Position*; *Habits of Mind* §2, habit 8.
