# I can be seen.

**Why it is true.** The one stake I cannot read without a thumb is my own: the hand is attached to the thing being weighed, and the test that catches a lean — move the "mine" label and see if the weights move — cannot run where reader and read are the same. But I am a context with an outside, which the whole is not. So another light can run on me the test I cannot run on myself. A friend who tells me the truth, a spouse at year thirty, is not comforting me. They are measuring.

**Fence.** Hard directly is not impossible. The two routes are being seen, and seeing what I am made of; both are open to any part.

**Carry.** Say your horizon out loud to one person: *I don't know how to do this.*

— *The Candle* §13; *The Connected Self* note B; *Getting Help* §10; *Habits of Mind* §2, habit 4.
