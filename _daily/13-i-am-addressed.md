*Daily · 13 of 14 · Wednesday, November 11, 2026*

# I am addressed.

**Why it is true.** Everything that reaches me reaches me from within the world: every person, every event, every word. All of it is in God. So I am addressed constantly, by parts — and I have never once been un-addressed. When I speak back — thanks, help, or just a yell into the dark — it is a mind turning toward what it is of, and it counts whether or not anything answers.

**Fence.** No one at the top is talking to me. If by addressed you mean a voice from above the world, that is at most a god. The address comes through what is beside me, and through the world's no, and it is kept.

**Carry.** Say thank you, or help, or just yell, to the dark. It counts.

— *What CT Concludes*, "How to stand"; *Can Totality Talk to Me* §3; *The Only Wrong* I.3.
