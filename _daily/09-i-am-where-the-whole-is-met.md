# I am where the whole is met.

**Why it is true.** The whole is reached only through particulars. There is no further place behind the person in front of me where God is more present; nowhere in the world is more happening than at a table where one person sees another. So for whoever is in front of me, I am the particular — the place the whole is met. And they are that for me.

**Fence.** If by this you mean I am the whole, no: a part with an edge is a god, not God. I am one of the places God is happening, and so is she, and so, a little, is the dog.

**Carry.** *Am I dealing with her, or with my account of her?*
