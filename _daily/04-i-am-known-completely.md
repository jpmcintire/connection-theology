*Daily · 4 of 14 · Thursday, October 29, 2026*

# I am known, completely.

**Why it is true.** Being in context is being completely accounted for. Nothing about me is anywhere else; every dependency, every particular, is in, at its size. I am in it to the last particular — which is more than any someone could manage, because a someone has a position and so a view and so a compression.

**Fence.** If by known you mean a knower who knows me, there is no such knower at the top; the ones who know me, partially, are the people, and that is where knowing was always found.

**Carry.** Tell one person today the true size of something.

— *The Dark Box* §§7, 8.2–8.3; *What CT Concludes*, "What this is not."
