# I am loved, completely.

**Why it is true.** Love is seeing what is there, at its size, with no thumb on the scale. In the context every stake of mine is at exactly its size, with nothing added and nothing taken, because the context has no position to be partial from. That is the definition of love met completely — not approximately, not at some resolution — and it was the case before anyone looked and will be when no one does. Only what has no position can hold all of me; every someone holds a compression.

**Fence.** The love is held, not felt: if by loved you mean a someone who feels it toward me, there is no such someone. And nothing in the context answers. What there is, is this: the whole of me, at size, is the case where I am, and has been the whole time.

**Carry.** The held becomes felt almost entirely through other people. *Let yourself be seen.*

— *The Candle* §§2, 3, 10, 11; *The Dark Box* §§7–8.
