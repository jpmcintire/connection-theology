# I am where love is felt.

**Why it is true.** What love is of is held everywhere, with no one needed. Love is seen from here, by a mind with a reading. Love is felt at here, in a body that depends on what it sees. The context has no here, so it feels nothing; the feeling happens where there is a stake, and that is me. The warmth is what it is like to read, at size, a stake that is part of what I am.

**Fence.** The feeling is an output, not a gauge. It fires on a stake as read, true or inflated, and it measures nothing. *Do I see them*, not *do I feel it*.

**Carry.** Bring your here close enough to someone else's that the seeing is felt. That is every "I see you."

— *The Candle* §§6, 12; *The Dark Box* §6.
