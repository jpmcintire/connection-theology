*Daily · 2 of 14 · Tuesday, October 27, 2026*

# I am held.

**Why it is true.** Three things, none a hope. I am in: there is no outside to be held out of. My stakes have sizes — what depends on me, what I depend on, what I carried, what I got wrong, what I repaired — and no one's position gets into a size. And the context has no hand: no "mine" in it to lean toward, no stranger to lean away from. The holding is not earned, is never withdrawn, and is not at risk.

**Fence.** If by held you mean a someone whose arms these are, there is no such someone. There is the place, and I am in it.

**Carry.** *What is doing this for me right now that I did not do?*

— *The Candle* §10; *What CT Concludes*, "You"; *Habits of Mind* §2, habit 1.
