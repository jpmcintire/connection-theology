# I am made of what reached me.

**Why it is true.** What reaches a part can only be other parts, and a part's account is made of what reached it. So I am made of what reached me: the people, the house, the language, the sentence someone said in 1987. My mother's hand is in my hand — not behind me, as a cause that finished, but in me, now, the way the tide is in the anemone. I am mostly other people, and that is more mine than any pilot could have made it, because all of me did it.

**Fence.** Connected is not dissolved. A self is a context with an outside, and the edge is real; that is why I can act, love, take sides, and die.

**Carry.** *Who can see what I can't, and why haven't I asked them?*

— *What CT Concludes*, "You"; *The Connected Self* §§0, 2, 4; *Getting Help* §4; *Habits of Mind* §2, habit 4.
