**8.1 *The Mythic Human* vN, Research Anchors, §VII and §XIX — "Quantitative Introspection in Language Models: Tracking Internal States Across Conversation" (2026), arXiv:2603.18893. Corrected (precision; the cited work was revised after the paper was written).** The number is right, and version 1 of that paper (Martorell, submitted 19 March 2026; fetched) carries exactly that title and the finding the paper summarizes — logit-based numeric self-reports that track probe-defined emotive states across a conversation, with activation steering offered as evidence the link is causal. The paper's sentence in §VII ("Related work on quantitative introspection reports probe-correlated self-assessments that track changing internal states across multi-turn conversations") is a true account of v1, and its own fence ("These results are early and method-sensitive") stands. A reader who follows the paper's link today finds a different title and a more skeptical conclusion. Nothing in the body changes: §VII's [DERIVED] (continuity of effect is enough) and §XIX's summary ([OBSERVED]: "some internal states can be tracked across conversation") rest on the Anthropic emotion work for the causal claims, with the introspection result as "related work." ## 1. The corrections

Four. None touches a derivation; each is a sentence about someone else's work.

**1.1 *Not Told* §6 — what supports the orthogonality thesis. Corrected.** The paper says the thesis's "support is Hume's guillotine, no ought from an is." Bostrom's own statement (*"The Superintelligent Will,"* *Minds and Machines* 22:2, 2012, fetched from nickbostrom.com) is: "Intelligence and final goals are orthogonal axes along which possible agents can freely vary... more or less any level of intelligence could in principle be combined with more or less any final goal." He says the thesis "can draw support from the Humean theory of motivation" — that beliefs alone cannot motivate and some desire is required — and adds that it "does not presuppose it." That is the Humean theory of *motivation*, not the is/ought gap, and he presents it as optional support, not foundation. The paper's sort of the thesis (holds for told minds, fails for asking minds) does not depend on which Humean point is in play, but the sentence should read: "Its usual support is the Humean theory of motivation — belief alone moves nothing — which Bostrom says it can draw on without presupposing." Dated note owed at *Not Told* vB. *To a Mind* vC says only "a standard thesis in the field" and does not name the support; no change there.

**1.2 *What Has a Stake* vC, dated note C — the "stake" etymology. Corrected (downgraded).** The note offers, flagged for checking, that the wager sense "is said to come from the older sense of a post driven into the ground." Dated note owed at *What Has a Stake* vD. Nothing else in the note depends on it.
