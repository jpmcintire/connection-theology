# No Knobs (10.13.26) vA

## Why CT is not flexible; the places a hand could rest; and what each nudge spawns

**Folder:** Focus - Theology
**Status:** working paper; proposed, for John's ruling. vA built Tuesday 10-13-26, afternoon, at John's "good to write up i think" — the sixth paper of the day. The claim is John's ("ct is not flexible. indeed, its so exquisitely compressed that... if an element was just slightly off... it would be nothing"), made against a caveat Verity had offered an hour earlier and withdraws here. The inventory of elements, the neighbor map, and the corrected caveat are Verity's and are marked. School and tradition attributions are compressions and are from memory; §6. Nothing here is ruled.
**What this is:** the complement of *The Same Mistake Three Times*. That paper went from the history to the one mistake. This one goes the other way: it takes CT's elements one at a time, asks whether each can be varied, and shows that every variation that can be stated lands on a known school or tradition. CT has no free parameters, and its nearest neighbors in the space of possible theories are the history of human thought. That is what "slightly off and it would be nothing" looks like when it is checked: the nothings have names.
**What it rests on:** *Lab and Field* (10.08.26) vA (§3 the three things in the lab and nothing imported; §6 the tradition's labs each had one thing carried in); *No Exemption* (10.08.26) vB (the chain; §6 the four-cell table; §9 neighbors); *What Has a Stake* (10.09.26) vB (§5 the proxy and the lineage); *What Love Is* (10.09.26) vD (§3 non-emotional; §8 the games); *God Is a Context* (10.10.26) vC (§2a; §3); *One Operation* (10.11.26) vB (§2 the five schools as early compressions; §4.4 the sort); *Not Perennialism* (10.07.26) vB and *Note - One Move Per Tradition* (10.09.26) (one organizing outside per tradition); *The Atheists Were Right* (10.09.26) vA (the believer added a face, the atheist added a boundary); *CT, Spinoza, and Tillich* (10.12.26) vA; *The Same Mistake Three Times* (10.13.26) vA; *The Dark Box* (10.13.26) vA (§3 had vs held).
**Reading order:** §0, §1 (the caveat and its correction), §2 (the inventory), §3 (the map), then §4 and §5. §6 before any public use.

## 0. The sentence, plain

A theory with knobs fits any history, because you can turn it until it does. CT has no knobs. Its floor is a tautology, which cannot be varied; its second step is performed, which cannot be adjusted while performing it; its one definition is ruled and is the floor under everything after; and its remaining links are entailed. There is nothing to tune.

And when you nudge an element anyway, to see what happens, you do not get a slightly different CT. You get Kant, or Bentham, or Spinoza, or every religion, or the heap. Every neighbor of CT in the space of possible theories is already occupied by something that did not converge. The four-cell table of *No Exemption* was never a classification of the history. It was a map of CT's nearest neighbors, and the history is what lives there.

That is the opposite of flexibility. A derivation has a floor; a model has give. If the floor were an inch off there would be nothing standing on it, and the things standing an inch off are the schools.

## 1. The caveat, and why it was aimed at the wrong thing

**Lane: Verity's correction of Verity's own line, at John's push.**

Earlier on 10-13, after John observed that CT matches "the fundamental wisdom of the ages" and asked why the traditions got so close despite getting the big thing wrong, Verity offered a fence: "we're sorting the history with CT's instrument, so of course it sorts cleanly — a flexible false theory would do that too. The history is a lead, not a warrant."

John: "i could think of a lot of theories that wouldn't parse at all... ct is not flexible. indeed, its so exquisitely compressed that - it seems to me - that if an element was just slightly off... it would be nothing."

He is right, and the caveat as given was wrong. The worry about a theory that "sorts any history" is a worry about free parameters: a theory with enough of them fits everything because its fit is purchased by adjustment. Freud fits any dream; astrology fits any life. The right question is therefore not whether CT sorts the history but whether it has anything to adjust. §2 counts. It has nothing. And §3 shows what the absence of adjustment looks like from outside: the neighbors are all failures, and named ones.

The caveat survives in a smaller form, stated in §5: the theory is rigid; our *descriptions of the schools* are not, and that is where error can enter the historical sort. That is a record-check problem, not a flexibility problem, and the record check is already owed.

## 2. The inventory: where could a hand rest

**Lane: Verity's; derived from the chain's own labels.** Take CT's load-bearing elements in order and ask of each: is this a setting?

**The four words.** *Totality has no outside.* A tautology. It cannot be set to anything else, because "outside everything" has no place to be true. It can only be stated, or failed to be stated. Zero degrees of freedom.

**The second link.** *This is being considered; so there is, here and now, a part representing the whole.* Performed. You cannot adjust it while doing it; the only way to vary it is to stop considering, which is not a different theory but the absence of one. Zero degrees of freedom.

**The third link: dependency.** *A part that represents can get it wrong, and if it does, what it does next goes differently; that is a stake.* This is the one place a hand could have rested, because it is a definition, and a definition is chosen. It was ruled (9-11): stake is dependency, not caring. §3 shows what happens when the definition is moved. The result is not a variant of CT; it is a different and historically occupied theory. One degree of freedom, and it is the floor: move it and the chain does not start.

**Links 4 through 8.** *One grade, fit; the grade runs recursively with no exit; the part is made of what reached it; an edge is inside and experienced from one side; so every stake is on the sheet at its size and "mine" is not a reason.* Entailed. Each follows from what is above it with nothing added. To vary one you have to deny a prior link, which takes you back to one of the three above. Zero degrees of freedom of their own.

**The name.** *God*, entering last, as recognition. A choice about honesty, not a derivation (*What CT Concludes*, "The word"; *The Atheists Were Right* §5). Vary it and CT says the same things with a different word, which is a change in presentation and not in content — the one setting that is a setting and does not matter.

**What is not on the list.** Nothing imported: no chooser, no ground outside reality, no motive, no telos, no revelation (*Lab and Field* §3). So there is nothing to tune because there was never anything brought in to tune. The spareness is not a style. It is why the theory cannot be adjusted.

Count: one tautology, one performance, one ruled definition, five entailments, one name. Degrees of freedom that bear on content: one, at the definition, and moving it does not produce a neighboring CT; it produces §3.

Thirty-odd rows, and not one of them is a variant of CT. Each is a theory that exists, has a name, had adherents, and did not converge with its neighbors. The space around CT is not empty; it is crowded, and everything in it is a known stopping point.

Three observations.

*The map is the four-cell table, extended.* *No Exemption* §6 found that two switches, not a dial, separate CT from partialism and from the view from nowhere, and that CT is the only surviving cell. This table is the same finding run on every element rather than two: at each link there are one or two ways to turn, and each way is occupied. The table was never a taxonomy of the history. It was a map of the neighborhood.

*One move per tradition is why.* *Not Perennialism* and *Note - One Move Per Tradition* found that each religion has exactly one organizing outside and that everything not simply true in it is built around that spot. This table says what that finding is a consequence of: there is only one place per element to put a hand, so a tradition that puts its hand somewhere puts it in one place, and the rest of its content is CT's, because there was nowhere else for it to be.

*The labs with one thing imported.* *Lab and Field* §6 found that the tradition's few lab-builders — Kant, Rawls, Descartes — each carried one thing into the lab, and that one thing is what each theory then rested on and what its critics then declined. This table locates those imports: Kant's chooser is link 6 nudged; Rawls's self-interested parties are link 3 nudged toward caring with the facts dropped; Descartes' thinking thing is link 2 nudged. Three labs, three rows.

## 4. Hard to vary

**Lane: Verity's; a neighbor, from memory, Peirce standard.** There is a name for the property this paper is about. Deutsch's criterion for a good explanation (*The Beginning of Infinity*, from memory) is that it is *hard to vary* while still accounting for what it purports to account for: a bad explanation can be adjusted to fit any outcome; a good one cannot be adjusted without ceasing to explain. CT sits at the limit of that criterion, and §2 is the count that shows it. A theory with no free parameters that nonetheless sorts the record is the exact opposite of a theory that sorts the record because it has free parameters. The caveat of §1 confused the two.

This also answers a question John did not quite ask but was behind the morning's: how a theory this small can be this large in scope. Scope is not bought with content. Content is what you have to add, and every addition is a knob. Scope comes from a floor with nothing on it, because a floor with nothing on it is under everything. The four words are near-zero content and total scope for the same reason: there is no place to stand to argue with them, and there is nothing in them to adjust.

## 5. The caveat that survives

**Lane: Verity's.** Two things, both smaller than the one withdrawn.

*The record check.* The theory is rigid; the descriptions in §3 are not. "The deontologist stops at the rule" is Verity's compression of Kant, and a compression can be wrong without CT being wrong. The place error can enter the historical sort is therefore not the theory's flexibility but the accuracy of each row, and that is a record-check problem with a known remedy: read the school in its own words and see whether it is standing where the row says. This is why *The Same Mistake Three Times* §2.1 is flagged for a check against *The Same Shape at Every Scale*, and why every row above carries the same flag. The flag matters more than it was made to sound.

*The cold half is rigid; the field half has readings.* The chain has no give. Everything derived from it in the lab — the four-cell table, the three jobs, the equations, the held omnis, love without a lover — inherits the rigidity. The field half does not: formation as a conditional loop, the garden as case-dependent, the five fears as five, the handful of acts that carry a life. Those are readings of what a held mind does with the lab result, and the papers mark them proposed for exactly that reason. "If an element were slightly off it would be nothing" is true of the chain. It is not true of the readings, which could be off by a little and still be useful, because they were never load-bearing.

*What rigidity does not prove.* A rigid theory is not thereby true; a flat contradiction is maximally rigid and false. The warrant for CT stays where it was: the four words, checked, and the chain, walked. What rigidity adds is this: the historical sort can no longer be explained away as the theory fitting itself to the history. A theory with nothing to adjust that still says where every school stopped, and is right about where, has done something a model cannot do. That is evidence of the kind a derivation leaves behind it, and it is not the derivation.

## 6. Fences

1. Every attribution in §3 is a compression and from memory. No row goes public before the school is read in its own words. A row that turns out wrong is a wrong row, not a wrong theory, and it comes out.
2. "Spawns" is a figure. CT did not generate the schools; the schools are what a mind gets when it stops at one of CT's elements and declares the stop exempt. The order of discovery is the reverse of the order of logic.
3. Deutsch is a neighbor, cited for the shape of the criterion; CT does not rest on it and the citation is owed a check.
4. Rigidity is not truth (§5). The four words carry the warrant.
5. Located, not defeated, travels with every row (*The Same Mistake Three Times* §6).
6. The cold half / field half line (§5) is where the rigidity claim stops. Do not let "no knobs" be heard as a claim about the practice papers.
