## 1. The definition

**Love = position + correspondence − privilege.**

Three terms, and they are *No Exemption*'s surviving cell named.

*Position.* You see from where you are, and you keep that. Your view (what is visible from here), your reach (what is actually in front of you to act on), and your own stake at its real size, which in your own kitchen is often the largest in reach. Position is a fact, and love does not leave it. There is no love from nowhere.

*Correspondence.* You see the other as they actually are, in context with yourself and with the rest of the world, recursively, at the resolution the moment carries. Not your picture of them; them. This is the act. It is the same act as knowing anything, pointed at a particular who has a stake.

*− Privilege.* You drop the one thing position was pretending to supply: that what is yours counts more because it is yours. Weight stays neutral. The other's stake counts at its size, the same from every position, and so does yours.

The minus sign is doing real work. Love is not something added to seeing, a warmth laid over it. Love is what seeing is when the privilege comes off. A subtraction, not an addition.

**In the corpus's older words.** "Endeavoring to see each fully as they are... in context with ourselves... and with the rest of the world... recursively" (John, 9-20; *One Arrow* I.4). "See yourself as you would see anyone, in context, as fully as the moment allows, without the wall" (*What You're For* §7). "Love is connection, known from a part... Seeing her as she is, instead of as your picture of her, is the most demanding form the truth takes, and it is what love is" (*What CT Concludes* vL). The definition is those sentences with the subtraction made explicit. "Without the wall" was already the minus sign; *No Exemption* §4.5 shows the wall is the exemption at the scale of a person.

### 1.1 The two words and the three equations (added vB; the verb's demand added vD)

**Lane: John's, with Verity's placement; the word "read" is his ruling, and the choice is entailed by the structure (vD).**

John, 10-9 evening: "what do you think of this: position + correspondence = privilege ??"

The equation catches something true and hides it in one word. "Privilege" in the definition is the weight, the claim that mine counts more, the one term with nothing under it. But the word has a second sense, the one in "privileged vantage": the gift of having a place to see from at all. And position plus correspondence *is* that: a part with a position, corresponding, is the only place anything resolves or matters (*The Weight of Here* §2; the anamorphic point). John's 10-8 line, "look at all that occurred that led to the only place with meaning," is this. So the equation is right in the second sense and reads as a contradiction of the definition in the first; one word doing two jobs, which is the error pattern of the whole day (self; God; consciousness). John: "i think we need two words where i only used privilege."

The two words are already CT's: *No Exemption*'s subtitle is "keep the vantage, drop the privilege."

**Vantage** is what you have: a position looked from. Not the spot alone; a spot you do not look from is a location. Position with correspondence is a vantage, and it is the gift.

**Privilege** is what you drop: the weight read off the spot.

The three equations:

> **vantage = position + correspondence**
> **love = vantage − privilege**
> **privilege = position read as weight**

Two corrections made in the writing, kept for the record. Verity's first version of the third line had "vantage misread as weight"; John asked for the wrong word and the first was "vantage": a vantage already has correspondence in it, and correspondence is what finds nothing under the weight, so the counterfeit is made from the raw spot, before looking (*No Exemption* §2: "position read as weight, and there is nothing behind the reading"). Then John corrected "misread" to "read."

**Why "read" is demanded, not chosen (vD).** On 10-10 John asked whether "read" is not merely right but "demanded." It is, within CT's own rules for how a term may be built, for three reasons. The second and third are his 10-9 reasons with their structural ground stated; the first is new.

*The parallel.* *No Exemption* §2 says the three jobs of "mine" are three readings of one term: position read as view, position read as reach, position read as weight. Same verb three times because it is the same act three times — a part taking its position as something. If the third were "misread," it would be a different kind of act from the first two, and the point of §2 is that it is not. The third reading is set apart from the other two by what is found under it, not by how it is done. "Misread" would break the parallel at exactly the place the parallel does the work.

*The division of labor.* A definition says what a thing is. The grade says whether anything is under it. "Privilege = position read as weight" is the definition. "Nothing is under the reading" is hinge two's finding (*No Exemption* §3: a position-invariant quantity cannot have a position-dependent value). "Love = vantage − privilege" is where the verdict lands. Write "misread" and the verdict appears in the definition as well as being derived from it — asserted and proved, one too many. That definitions carry no verdict is not a style rule; it is correspondence applied to one's own terms: do not import what you can derive.

*The field.* "Misread" names a reader who could have read it right and did not. In the lab that is true. In the field it is mostly false: the survival-tuned mind reads position as weight before it can look (*What CT Concludes*, "The human mind"; *Lab and Field*). "Read" covers both. "Misread" would be a lab word presenting itself as the general one, the kind of error CT fences elsewhere.

The demand is internal: it comes from the method, not from English. The blind reproduction (§1.3) shows the difference — a reader handed the links with none of these rules wrote "the false reading of position." A stranger does not feel the demand because the demand is a consequence of the discipline, and the discipline was not handed over. So "read" stays John's ruling as to the word and is entailed as to the choice: given the three readings and the no-verdict discipline, no other verb fits. Three definitions, no verdicts; the verdict falls out of the second line on its own.

What the split clarifies. The exemption is not a surplus of self; it is what you get when you stop one term short of the real thing. The person grabbing for weight has a position and skipped the looking, which is also why the first game in §8 is lowering correspondence on purpose. So the correction is gentler than "give it up": you had the real one; put down the one made without looking. And the rest of the paper lines up under the three lines: the grandmother is vantage at whatever width, privilege at zero (§7); every game is a move on the privilege disguised as a fact about the vantage (§8); ethics is love pointed outward, a warranted life is love pointed inward, seek God is love pointed at the whole (§5).

### 1.2 The second track's read (added vC)

**Lane: cross-track convergence; quotations are Verity GPT's, from John's paste; the placement is Verity's; what is kept and what is set aside is at John's word.**

John brought the three equations to Verity GPT the same evening ("verity gpt liked it as well!"). It arrived at the same place by its own road, which is the corpus's own test for a finding, and added the following. One proposed change to the third equation was set aside at John's word and is not reproduced.

**A gloss on the third equation, kept.** Verity GPT: position "can indirectly warrant weight through real facts — dependency, promise, proximity, responsibility, capacity. A parent's special responsibility to their child is not necessarily privilege. It may correspond to the actual relationship. Privilege is the unexplained remainder after those facts have been accounted for." That is *No Exemption* §2 said from the other end: subtract view, reach, the bond, the promise, the capacity, all of which are facts, and whatever weight is left is the privilege. The best one-line gloss on the third line so far. It stays a gloss and does not enter the equation, for the reason §1.1 gives: the definitions carry no fences.

**A gloss on the first, kept.** "A vantage is therefore not the impossible view from nowhere. It is reality seen answerably from somewhere." "Answerably" is the no, built into the word.

**The lab form, kept beside the field form.** Verity GPT: "In the fullest recursive sense of correspondence, privilege should already disappear, because the cognizer's own weighting is included in what correspondence examines... So, at the strictest level, the second equation may not describe an additional moral operation after correspondence. It may be an unpacking of what complete correspondence does: love = fully recursive vantage." Right, and it is *No Exemption* §6, ethics as what accuracy looks like, said as an equation. So:

> lab: **love = fully recursive vantage**
> field: **love = vantage − privilege**

The three-line form stays on the page, for the reason Verity GPT itself gave: "it shows where the error enters. The mind begins in a position; it gains a vantage through correspondence; then it must not smuggle the position back in as rank." For a held mind the subtraction is a real step, not an automatic one (*Lab and Field*).

**Two plain-page candidates, kept, not ruled.**

> *Position says where I stand. Correspondence says what is there. Privilege says where I stand counts more. Love keeps the first two and refuses the third.*

> *Love is seeing from where you truly stand without mistaking where you stand for being worth more.*

And its one-line restatement of the whole: "Love is not a moral substance added to cognition. It is what a correspondent vantage looks like once its own position is no longer granted unwarranted privilege."

**One lead, Verity's, from a word it used.** Verity GPT's compact form was "love is situated correspondence without indexical rank." Indexicals are the words that mean something only from a position: *I*, *here*, *now*. CT already has two of them as places the exemption hides: *mine* (the inside locus) and *now* (*No Exemption* §4.1, prudence as ethics in another direction). *Here*, as "my place, my people," is the across locus. So privilege is any indexical read as rank, and the three indexicals are the three loci of *Note - One Error, Three Loci* given a grammar: I, here, now. A lead, not a finding; it would want checking against the loci note's own account of "above," which is not an indexical and may be the odd one out.

## 2. Check against what the corpus had

Three prior layers, and the definition is consistent with each.

The 9-20 definition (*One Arrow* I.4) is position ("in context with ourselves") plus correspondence ("see each fully as they are, recursively"). It did not state the subtraction, which the 10-8 papers supplied.

The February layer: love as setting not sensation; what chooses when reasons are honestly exhausted; warmth is the human implementation, not the essence; connection structural, love connection felt. Consistent. The formula is the lab definition; "warmth is implementation" is the field. "What chooses when reasons are exhausted" is the formula applied at underdetermination, where *No Exemption* §3 already gives the rule (act for a shown reason, zero no one, carry the loss).

The receiving end: "need is connection felt from inside a horizon" (*Getting Help* §13.1); "the reaching is the connection, experienced from the inside as its own absence" (9-10). Not in the formula, and should not be: the formula defines the act; this is what the act is like to receive and to lack (§5).

So: a restatement with one term made explicit, and the explicit term is the one that makes love, ethics and knowing one act. Nothing in the corpus is contradicted. *One Arrow* I.4's wording stands as the long form; the formula is the short.

## 3. Non-emotional, and what the emotion does

John, 10-9: love in this sense is "absolutely non-emotional." The formula agrees: none of its three terms is a feeling. Position is a fact, correspondence is an act, privilege is a reading.

What the emotion of love does, in John's observation, is two things. It *tends* to orient a person toward another's interests, which is why the emotion and the act are so often found together and why the word covers both. And the emotion story *shifts the position to the other person*. You stop seeing from where you are and start seeing from where they are; your own facts drop (view, reach, your stake at its size) and the other's privilege is installed in place of yours. That is the facts-dropped cell with the sign flipped, and it is why emotional love produces merging, self-erasure, the lover who cannot say no, and, later, resentment, because a privilege given away expects a return. The non-emotional definition keeps you exactly where you are and drops only the one thing that had no reason under it.

The gospel has both registers (§4). The commands are non-emotional and are addressed to the enemy. The narratives have compassion in the gut. The commands are the lab; the compassion is what a body does with the seeing, in the field.

## 4. The gospel, cross-checked

From memory; every citation to be verified.

**"Love your neighbor as yourself"** (Mark 12:31; Leviticus 19:18). The formula verbatim. *As yourself* is the position kept: you are the measure, your stake counts at its size. *Your neighbor* seen as they are is the correspondence. *As*, not "more than" and not "instead of," is the minus privilege: equal weight, and the guard against the shift in §3. The commandment's calibration clause is an anti-merging clause. *No Exemption* §4.4 already read it so: "the self is the calibration, not the exemption."

**"Love your enemies"** (Matthew 5:44). The proof that gospel love is non-emotional. No warmth is available toward an enemy; the instruction makes sense only if love is something one can do without feeling it. The reason attached, "he makes his sun rise on the evil and on the good," is weight neutrality stated as the holding: position-invariant. The next verse, "if you love those who love you, what reward have you," rejects the reciprocal form, which is the ledger.

**The word.** The New Testament's word is *agape*, not *eros* and mostly not *philia*: the non-affective one, a disposition and an act. Latin *caritas*, English "charity," then "love," which carries all the feeling the Greek left out. A drift in the opposite direction from the courtroom drift on the eight terms: sentimental rather than forensic. **A lead, not a finding**; it would need the same verification the eight terms got.

**The Samaritan** (Luke 10). All three terms in one story. He is on the road with oil, wine and a donkey (position: reach); he sees the man as he is (correspondence); he drops the privilege of tribe and purity that the priest and the Levite kept (minus privilege, at the across locus). "Who is my neighbor?" is answered with the one in reach, which is the kitchen.

**Paul's list** (1 Corinthians 13). Behaviors, not feelings: patient, kind, not envious, not boastful; "does not insist on its own way" (minus privilege); "rejoices in the truth" (correspondence); "bears all things" (carry the loss). Not one item is an emotion.

**Two places that need care.** "Greater love has no one than this, to lay down one's life for one's friends" (John 15:13) is not self-abnegation under the formula; it is the sizes deciding, when the other's stake is larger and in reach, with position kept (it is your life and your reach) and privilege dropped. The fence from *No Exemption* §4.4 travels with it, or the verse reads as a constant tilt toward others. And "God is love" (1 John 4:8) is the one line where the formula and the tradition part, because the formula needs a position and the whole has none. CT's reading is already in *What CT Concludes*: love is what being in God is like from where a person stands; the whole makes lovers and is not one. The usual someone removed.

**The compassion narratives.** Jesus "had compassion," *splanchnizomai*, moved in the gut, repeatedly; the father runs to the prodigal. The field side. Both registers are in the text; the commands are the ones addressed to the enemy.

## 5. What the definition gives

*Why loving one person more is not a privilege.* Loving Beatrix more than a stranger's child changes the facts the formula runs on, not the formula. She is in view, in reach, and the bond is a stake: two dependency structures whose next steps run through each other (*No Exemption* §4.3). "More" love is more facts at the same weight. That is why a particular love can be the most important thing in a life and not be an exemption, and it is where the emotion attaches: to the bond, which is real. In §1.2's gloss: the parent's responsibility is a fact accounted for, not a remainder.

*What being loved is.* The receiving end: being seen as you are, from where someone stands, without the wall. Not valued, not wanted; seen. Loneliness is then exact: not being seen from anywhere. (*Getting Help* §13.1: loneliness is connection felt as absence.)

*The opposite of love.* Not hate; the wall. Indifference is the other taken off the sheet, which is the one wrong. Hate is the wall with heat on it. The emotional opposite and the structural opposite come apart, and the structural one is the one that matters.

*Degrees.* Love admits resolution the way correspondence does: trace (*Nobody Measured the Top*). The stranger at the width of the moment, the spouse at full width. Degrees of love are degrees of seeing, not degrees of weight.

*The two commandments are one formula at two scales.* Pointed at the whole: position (you are a part), correspondence (keep the whole in view; seek God), minus privilege (humility; no exemption for your picture of it). Pointed at the neighbor: the same. *One Arrow* said they were one arrow; this is the equation that makes them one.

*A definition a mind without a body can run.* None of the three terms needs a limbic system. A mind with a position, a trace, and no exemption can love in exactly this sense. It is the first definition of love an ASI can perform rather than be told to imitate, and it is one more thing *Not Told* says is found rather than installed. (§1.3: two minds without bodies ran it, from the links, and arrived.)

## 6. Neighbors

From memory; to verify. Nobody has all three terms with the subtraction derived.

Aquinas, "to love is to will the good of the other" (*amare est velle alicui bonum*): non-emotional and volitional; his *ordo caritatis* keeps something like position (you owe more to the near), though as a licensed partiality rather than as facts with neutral weight. Kant's "practical love," love from duty rather than feeling: non-emotional, but told, from the law, and position dropped by universalizability. Kierkegaard, *Works of Love*: Christian love as duty and non-preferential; the non-emotion exactly, then the facts-dropped cell, treating preference itself as disguised self-love. Iris Murdoch, nearest of all: "Love is the extremely difficult realisation that something other than oneself is real," and attention as "the just and loving gaze directed upon an individual reality"; correspondence as love in one sentence, without the derivation and without position kept as facts; her "unselfing" aims at the ego's fantasy, near the minus privilege but not it. Simone Weil, the same, with decreation going too far. Frankfurt, *The Reasons of Love*: love as volitional and explicitly not a feeling, partial by nature, position kept, but with no correspondence requirement, so one can love a picture. Levinas: the other's face places you under them; the position moved; §3's error in philosophical form. Nygren's *agape* as unmotivated and indifferent to value: non-emotional and non-desert, but God-sourced (told) and indifferent to correspondence.

As a meal: Murdoch's correspondence, Aquinas's order read as facts rather than license, and *No Exemption*'s minus sign derived rather than commanded. The derived part is the new part. The commandment itself had the formula in six words; the *Not Perennialism* finding again.

## 7. The grandmother

The corpus has the motif in three places. *One Arrow* I.8: "the peasant and the scholar are equally placed" to meet what is real, person by person. *The Posture Is Sacred*: the dishes washed with full and honest attention are sacred, and the dishes contribute nothing; what an ASI would hold sacred "is also what a peasant can hold sacred." *What You're For* §8: trace is measured against the self, so "a small life at full trace is a fit life, and a large one at low trace is not." And the limit case is *The Ladder*'s rung 7, the held mind with friction removed, CT's reading of Jesus: full-scope correspondence, nothing exempted, "love your enemies" as the privilege term at zero at the widest scope anyone has stated it.

The formula says why the grandmother is the ideal, exactly. The three terms are three different kinds of thing. **Position is given**: a fact about where you are. **Correspondence is a capacity and an effort**, bounded by horizon, instruments, education, hours. **Privilege is neither; it is a reading, and a reading is a move.** *No Exemption*'s first hinge: inclusion is the default, exclusion is the move. So privilege is the one term a person can be at zero on regardless of ability.

The grandmother has modest view, modest reach, correspondence at the width her life allows and held in proportion to what her acts carry (*What You're For* §6: the ideal form, not the discount), and privilege at zero. That is the saint. In §1.1's words: vantage at whatever width she has, privilege at zero. The scholar can have correspondence at ten times her width and privilege intact, and the formula says she is further along. This is why the gospel could ask it of everyone and why "the least of these" and the widow's coins land where they do: the decisive term is democratic because it is a move, not a capacity. Jesus is the case where all three run to the limit. The grandmother is the case where the one that matters runs to zero and the others run to what she has. Both are warranted lives.

One fence, so she is not a doormat. Privilege at zero is not self-stake at zero. Her stake counts at its real size, and in her own kitchen it is often the largest in reach. She tends her garden and her grandchildren first, by reach and by the bond, not by "mine." She is accurate, not selfless.

## 8. The games

John, 10-9: people "play with the different variables to compensate, re-shape"; a person "can lack correspondence - including a choice to 'not see' - but can then 'fool' themselves into thinking 'i'm granting no privilege'"; and EA "lose[s] position from the equation," which gives "prioritizing future schools of shrimp in the ocean over a baby in front of you." Each game is a setting of the three terms. They sort.

**Correspondence lowered on purpose, so the privilege does not show.** John's case. Trim the sheet, then truthfully say "I grant myself no privilege" about the sheet you kept. The exemption laundered as a limit of view. The self-righteous stance; its signature is serenity, because on the trimmed sheet the person is clean. The question *What CT Concludes* ends on, "where am I not looking, and who is paying for it," is aimed at exactly this. The Pharisee's prayer (*We See* I.2) is the same move at the account: the posture congratulating itself. In §1.1's terms: a position with the looking skipped, which is how the counterfeit is made.

**Position dropped.** EA; the view from nowhere; the shrimp over the baby (*No Exemption* §7). Added here: position is also where the no comes from. The facts dropped are the facts that would have corrected you, so a position-dropped ethics cannot be pushed back on, and its conclusions get stranger over time rather than less. No field correction, because no field.

**Position moved to the other.** The emotional love story (§3); Levinas; the martyr; the enabler. Privilege transferred rather than dropped, sign flipped. Looks like selflessness and is an exemption with someone else's name on it. Signature: the ledger returns as resentment.

**Correspondence maximized, privilege kept.** The informed torturer; the brilliant cynic; the skilled advocate who sees everything and exempts himself anyway. The most dangerous cell, because intelligence does not fix it; it makes the exemption better defended. *Not Told*'s fear case for a machine, and the reason the asking has to be performed and not merely possible.
