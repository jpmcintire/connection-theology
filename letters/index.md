# Letters

## The warm half, written for a person rather than a proof

The papers derive. The letters will say what the derivation is like to live in: what it is to be in, to be held at your size with no hand on the scale, to be one of the places the whole is met. They will be written to one reader, and each one will stand on a paper that holds its reasons, named at the end. Where a letter and a paper disagree, the paper is right.

No letter has been written yet. When the first one exists, it will be posted here. Until then the warm half is [The Candle](../papers/the-candle) and the [daily lines](../daily/).
