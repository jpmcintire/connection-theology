# Letters

## The warm half, written for a person rather than a proof

The papers derive. The letters say what the derivation is like to live in: what it is to be in, to be held at your size with no hand on the scale, to be one of the places the whole is met. They are written to one reader, and each one stands on a paper that holds its reasons, named at the end. Where a letter and a paper disagree, the paper is right.

Letters are added here on Sundays. The first season is twelve. None has been posted yet; the [daily lines](../daily/) begin October 26, and the first letter follows them.
