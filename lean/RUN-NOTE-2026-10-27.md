## 0. What was asked

John, 10-27 afternoon: "would a lean run take a lot of tokens. can we just test what changed?" So this was a lean run in the other sense too: only the four things the morning's three papers added were typed, against the vG file as it stood, with no new carrier and no new axiom.

## 1. Result, plain

Fourteen new theorems. All fourteen compile under Lean 4.35.0 (core only, no Mathlib) and every one of them reports `does not depend on any axioms`. The one classical dependency in the file (`unique_no_outside`, from vA) is unchanged. The inventory went from forty-six theorems (forty-five axiom-free) to sixty (fifty-nine axiom-free).

Two of the fourteen are proved by `Iff.rfl`, which is Lean's way of saying "the two sides are the same statement." That is the finding of the run, not a weakness of it. Each is below.

## 2. The four questions and what the file said

**Stakes tenseless, ethics a process (The Stopping Point §§1–2).** `Position` is already an arbitrary type, so a clock is any function out of it (`Clock`). The question was whether *when* a reading happens can enter partiality except through the hand. It cannot, and the reason is prior to clocks: `Partial` was always a predicate on the reading alone. `partial_depends_only_on_reading` is `Iff.rfl` — swap the position, keep the estimates and weightings, and the partiality claim does not change its text. `time_never_enters_partial` is the same theorem with a clock in the hypotheses doing nothing. The lateness discount, `lateness_discount_is_partial`, is `discount_for_reason_is_partial` with lateness as the reason, exactly as vF predicted it would be.

**The inquiry's own stake (The Stopping Point §3).** Whatever rides on how long the reading takes is `InquiryStake holder cost` — an ordinary `Stake`. It is `Held` by `rfl` and on the `WholeSheet` by `trivial`. Nothing is added to put it there; it was always on the sheet.

**Over-thinking and under-thinking as one wall (The Stopping Point §3.2; ruling 7).** Both are defined as `Excludes` — over-thinking excludes the inquiry's stake, under-thinking excludes a stake in view by stopping. `one_wall_two_directions` is `Iff.rfl`: the file cannot distinguish them except by *which* stake is zeroed. That is what "one error with the sign flipped" means when a type theory says it. Both are correspondence failures by the existing `excludes_not_correspondent`. The limit side of stopping — a stake the reader never had in view — is `unseen_not_excluded` again: dimness, not the hand. The stopping condition itself (`WarrantedClosure`) needs an order on `M`, which the file deliberately lacks (Report §3.2, "counting, not quantity"), so it is recorded as a definition under a declared order with no theorem resting on it. That is the same honest status as choice-under-conflict, and it is what the prose says: the form, not the number.

**Wielding across an edge; bounded (A Mind at the Top §6; Nothing Was Dialed §§2, 6).** `WieldsFrom C x y := C x ∧ ¬ C y` — to wield from a context on a thing is to have the thing outside the context. `totality_wields_nothing` is then one line from `trivial`, as cheap as the four words, which is what "derived in one move from the second word" means. `self_can_wield`: a context with an outside has something across its edge. `SetsFrom` has the same shape and `totality_sets_nothing` is the same theorem renamed — nothing was dialed from the whole. `Bounded := HasOutside`, so `totality_unbounded` is `totality_has_no_outside` under its other name, with nothing said about the cardinality of anything. That is the split in The God vC (10-1): unbounded is proved; infinite-as-quantity is physics.

## 3. What was not typed, and why

Wielding in the second sense — the spot where a reading becomes an act — still needs a type of actions, which the file does not have (Report §3.6). The loss proof of A Mind at the Top §6 ("a lossless representation of X is X; a mind at the top is a demotion") needs a resolution order on readings and is owed. Neither is a wall; both are open items with their shape known.

## 4. What the run cost

One toolchain download (Lean 4.35.0, about 60 seconds), two compiles (baseline vG, then vH), one fix (`at` is a reserved word in Lean; the clock's field is `time`). The whole thing was a fraction of a paper. Lean runs are cheap when the question is "did the new prose break the old types"; they are expensive only when the prose asks for a new carrier.
