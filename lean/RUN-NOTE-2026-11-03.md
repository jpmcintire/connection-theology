## 1. The occasion

*What a Stake Is Made Of* §9 listed six investigations for the file and said "No Lean source is altered or run by this revision." The afternoon's *Note - The Two Points of Attack* (11.03.26) vA §3.2 then said that until the run, the honest place to say the second point of attack stands is still at the definition, "with the note that the definition may have dissolved under it." This is the run.

The six, in the paper's order: represent the context of a dependency as a set of steps with the steps and the dependency relation stated, and say what that keeps and drops; keep inclusion within its demonstrated scope; keep the ontic dependency explicit by folding `D` into the stake's type; type the filing line; examine direction at the floor as a separation statement rather than a choice rule; separate structural comparison from action selection; and check the model against the argument, with "nothing added" as the standard to apply rather than a guarantee that an encoding has met it.

## 2. What changed in the file

One new section, §12a, between the toy frame and §13, plus a vJ paragraph in the header and one in §13. Twenty-nine new definitions and types (none replacing any); twenty-two new theorems; one `example` (a `Prop` type-checked as stateable). `Stake`, `Reading`, `Partial`, `Loving` and everything else above §12a: unchanged in statement and proof.

**(1) The filing line.** `Filer` — a thing (`holder`) holding a state (`held : S`) as of a thing (`about`), with `fits : S → W → Prop` saying when a state is right about a way the thing can be, and `next : S → Step` reading the next step off the state. `Filer.IsAccount` — the state can fit and can fail to fit (a state that fits every way, or none, is not about anything). `Filer.Guided` — some other state would give a different next step: the dependency of the step on what is held. `Filer.Files` — both. Toy: `thermostat` (states cold/warm as of the room; fits when it matches; heat when cold, idle when warm) — `thermostat_files`; `rock` (one state, fits everything) — `rock_not_account`, `rock_not_files`.

**(2) The edge.** `Edge` — `holder`, `on` (what it rides on), `magnitude`: the stake with the dependency in the type, neither field taking a `Position`. `Edge.toStake` forgets `on`. `EdgeD E` — the dependency relation a set of edges induces; this is §5a's parameter `D`, derived rather than supplied. Theorems: `edge_held`, `edge_on_sheet`, `unseen_edge_held` ("the asteroid no one has seen"), `edge_rides` (an edge is on `Meaning` under the derived `D`), `filer_edge_held`. `Filer.edge f m` — a filer's edge at a size; the size is not computed in the base frame, whose `M` has nothing to compute it in.

**(3) The region, as an experiment.** In its own section with its own carriers `Step` and `Dep a b` (step `b` depends on step `a`): `Region := Step → Prop`; `Region.empty`; `Downstream a` (reached from `a` by one or more dependencies — an inductive, so "following, not collecting"); `hangsOn a` (the region of a step: everything downstream); `Region.sub` (inclusion); `support w` (the region an effect profile `w : Step → Nat` determines); `firstTheSmaller`, `firstTheLarger` (two readings of a nested pair); `regionFrame` (`M := Region Step`, `zero := Region.empty`). Theorems: `hangsOn_closed` (a region is closed under the dependencies); `sub_refl`, `sub_trans`, `sub_antisymm` (inclusion is a partial order on regions — a fact about the chosen sets and nothing more); `hangsOn_sub_of_mem` (INCLUSION WHERE IT HOLDS: if `b` hangs on `a`, what hangs on `b` hangs on `a` — the one place inclusion arises from the dependencies rather than being imposed on them); `region_forgets_degree` (WHAT THE REPRESENTATION DROPS: two profiles with the same support give the same region and are not the same profile — which steps go differently is kept, how differently is dropped); `inclusion_underdetermines` (on any nested pair the inclusion fact is consistent with putting the smaller first and with putting the larger first; the proof is `⟨h, h⟩`, and that triviality is the point: a theorem about nested regions is not a theorem that dominated stakes lose); `region_held` (POSITION STILL DOES NOT CHANGE MAGNITUDE with the region as `M`: the same `rfl` as `all_held`). Outside the section, a two-step world: `incomparable_regions` (NOT A TOTAL ORDER: two regions neither of which includes the other, so inclusion cannot be a universal comparison rule). The `example`: `WarrantedClosure (regionFrame …) (Region.sub Step) gain cost` type-checks — the stopping condition's form is stateable with inclusion as the declared order; nothing is derived from it, and which `le` is right is not the file's to say. Every theorem in the file holds in the region frame, since every one is parametric in `F`.

**(4) Direction at the floor.** `Direction Step := Thing → Step → Prop` (for each thing, the steps its own filing moves it toward; a fact about the thing's dynamics, no `Position`). `RunsAgainst dir x st := ¬ dir x st` ("bad for" the holder as the complement of what it moves toward; position-free by type). `PreferenceStake` — a preference is a stake: `preference_held`, `preference_on_sheet`, the same `rfl` and `trivial` as the inquiry's stake. `Crosses dir me stepOf s` — the reason a reader might give: this stake crosses my direction. `direction_as_weight_is_partial` — DIRECTION AS A WEIGHT IS PARTIALITY: a reader that discounts a stake for crossing the reader's own direction, reading an equal stake that does not cross it at size, is partial, as an instance of `discount_for_reason_is_partial`; the direction is in the hypotheses and nowhere in the proof. That is the separation §7 asked for, as a theorem: the sign enters at the floor, as the holder's own stakes, and never as a multiplier on anyone else's.

**(5) Comparison is not selection.** `ActOn p` — an act: a move on a stake in reach (Report §6's "a type of actions"). `act_not_partial` — acting within reach is not partiality on its own: partiality is a fact about the reading, and an act adds nothing to it. `act_target_held` — an act does not change a size.

## 4. What the run settles, and what it does not

**Settles.** The filing line types, and the thermostat and the rock fall on the sides the paper put them (§5a). `Stake` is recoverable from `Edge` by forgetting what the edge rides on, and §5a's `D` is derivable from a set of edges, so the dependency no longer stands beside the stake as a separate parameter; an edge is held, on the sheet, and held when unseen, each by `rfl` or `trivial`. Inclusion of regions is a partial order; it arises from the dependencies exactly where a sub-dependency rides inside another; it is not total; it drops degree; it does not pick a direction of choice; and with the region as `M` every theorem in the file holds and the second hinge is the same `rfl`. A preference is a stake, and reading another's stake down because it crosses my direction is partiality by the one discount theorem. An act on a stake in reach is typeable and adds nothing to partiality or to size. The paper's §12b correction is confirmed from the formal side: inclusion is a relation available where it holds, and nothing in the file turns it into a comparison rule or an action verdict.

**Does not settle, and does not claim.** That a region *is* the size of a stake: the file can represent the claim and check what follows from the representation; it cannot check the identification. That every holder of a stake is a filer: `Stake` does not carry its filer, and the run does not make it. That `Filer.Files` is the right floor: it is a definition. Two places in it a critic should read: `about` is a field, declared, not checked — the file cannot say what a state is *of* in the world, exactly as `Account.about` and `TrueOfSource` were declared at vG; and `Guided` asks only that the next step depend on the state, not that the dependence run through the state's fit, so a thing whose step depends on its state for reasons unrelated to what the state is about still files. Those are the walls of this run, declared.

**So: the one declared definition did not become a theorem.** What changed is where the definition sits. `Stake` can now be built from an account of filing and dependency instead of standing alone, and the pieces it is built from are each checkable for what they say. The critic's place is now `Filer.Files` and `hangsOn`, in the open, as the paper's §8 said: definitions remain everywhere; there is one fewer that stands alone. For *Note - The Two Points of Attack* §3.2, the honest reading after the run is: the definition has not dissolved; it has decomposed, and the attack on it has to be an attack on the filing line or on the region, which are now written down where they can be attacked.

## 5. What this run says about the method

Sixth run; no narrowing. The five narrowings were each a case where the type said less or more than the prose and the prose already had the arm. This run found none, which is the expected result of a run that adds types beside the existing ones without touching them: there was nothing for the prover to disagree with except my proof mechanics. The content the run adds is in two theorems that say what a representation cannot do (`region_forgets_degree`, `inclusion_underdetermines`) and one that says where inclusion comes from when it is legitimate (`hangsOn_sub_of_mem`). A formalization that is honest about its representation should have theorems of that kind, and the file now does.
