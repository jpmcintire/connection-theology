## 1. The occasion

The 11-3 run note §4 declared two places in `Filer.Files` a critic should read: `about` is a field, declared and not checked; and `Guided` asks only that the next step depend on the state, not that the dependence run through the state's fit. The brief named both to the readers on purpose, with the instruction to aim at them rather than discover them. John's one-line report before the details: "i'm hearing both too broad and too narrow. do you want to think again before seeing any details?" My prediction, written before reading either reply and recorded in the session: too broad would be a thermostat-kind case, and that would count against the brief's wording of the claim rather than the definition; too narrow would be a reading of `next` as outward action. The first was right in kind and the second was right; neither predicted the hinged sign.

## 2. The cases, as sent, and their dispositions

The brief's rule: four dispositions, per case, and no merits verdict on the reader's reasoning — only the disposition and the compiled fact. One case needed a fifth disposition the brief did not anticipate, and it is named as such.

**Grok, "too wide" — the vending machine.** A coin-operated machine holding a credit state as of the coins inserted; the credit can match or fail to match; whether it dispenses depends on the credit. `vending_files` compiles as sent against vJ, and again at vK (its `fits` is injective in the state, so `ReadOff` is `readOff_of_fits_eq`). Grok's sentence on the word, as sent: "a vending machine does not think; it is a mechanical or electronic device that tracks inserted coins (possibly incorrectly) and releases a product or not according to that tracking." Disposition — not one of the four. The word goes the way the reader says, and the case files; but the case is the thermostat's kind, which the brief itself declared accepted as filing on purpose. So it is not a counterexample to `Files`; it is a counterexample to the brief's sentence "a thing thinks if and only if it files," which overstated what CT claims. CT's claim is that filing is the *floor* of thinking (*Nothing Added* §6), and ordinary usage draws the word higher than the floor. The finding is against the prose, and the wording is John's to rule on: whether CT says the vending machine "thinks" at the floor, or says "files" there and reserves "thinking" for what the floor supports. The file's §12a (7) records the question as John's.

**Grok, "too narrow" — the contemplator.** A person in pure contemplation, holding a thought as of a mathematical claim, correct or incorrect, whose next step is invariably the same (`next := fun _ => .continue`). `contemplator_not_files` compiles as sent: it is not `Guided`. Grok's sentence on the word, as sent: "a person quietly contemplating a mathematical claim, correctly or incorrectly, with no next action that depends on the claim, is still thinking." Disposition — *compiles and the word does not go that way*, with the reason in the construction rather than the word: as built, the held thought changes nothing, not even the next thought, and a thinker whose next thought does not depend on the thought held is a snapshot, not a thinker. The reader read `Step` as outward action; the file had not said otherwise, and that was a gap in the docstring, not the definition. The `Filer` docstring at vK says `Step` may be the filer's own next state. Rebuilt with the next step as the next thought — holding the claim as correct, press on; holding it as incorrect, look again; nothing outward either way — the same person files: `contemplator'_files`. Both the reader's case and the rebuild are in the file with credit.

**Verity GPT, "too wide" — the hinged sign. The sixth narrowing.** A hinged sign with a latch, thing 0, about the room, thing 1. The state is a pair: the printed temperature claim and the latch position. `fits` reads the first component against the room; `next` reads the second: engaged, the sign stays; released, it falls. `hingedSign_files` compiles as sent against vJ — the printed line can be right or wrong (`IsAccount`), and the latch gives a different step (`Guided`). GPT's second theorem, also as sent: two states that say the same thing about the room in every world, `(.cold, .engaged)` and `(.cold, .released)`, give different steps. As pasted, the reply carries the code and its docstrings; no verdict line or ordinary-word sentence came with it. None is needed: a sign does not think, and John's "totally agree" is the ruling. Disposition — *compiles and the word goes the way the reader says*: `Files` was too wide, at exactly the second declared weak point, and the file moves toward the word.

**At the first declared point.** Neither reader built a case there. After the round I built one against vK in the session (not in the file): a rock whose one variable, its own temperature, tracks the room's and drives whether it cracks — `about := 1`, `fits := fun s w => s = w`, `next`: cold → intact, warm → cracked. `hotRock_files` compiles; it passes the tightened clause too, because for it the state is the claim. Nobody says a rock that cracks when hot thinks. This is Verity's case, not a reader's, and it is reported as the first weak point standing open as declared, not as a seventh narrowing: the clause that would close it is not yet written (§5).

## 3. What changed in the file

**(1) The third clause.** `Filer.account f s` — the fit profile of a state, what it says about the thing. `Filer.ReadOff` — READ OFF THE ACCOUNT: two states with the same fit profile have the same next step (`∀ s s', (∀ w, f.fits s w ↔ f.fits s' w) → f.next s = f.next s'`). `Filer.Files := IsAccount ∧ Guided ∧ ReadOff`. `readOff_of_fits_eq` — when the fit relation is injective in the state (the state *is* the claim), `ReadOff` is automatic; this is how the thermostat, the vending machine and the rebuilt contemplator pass. The `Filer` docstring: `Step` may be the filer's own next state.

**(2) The outside round, in §12a (6).** `vending`, `vending_files`; `contemplator`, `contemplator_not_guided`, `contemplator_not_files`; `contemplator'`, `contemplator'_files`; `hingedSign`, `hingedSign_files_vJ` (vJ's two clauses, restated so the narrowing can be seen), `hingedSign_same_fit_different_step` (GPT's second theorem, as sent), `hingedSign_not_readOff` (the author's second theorem is the witness), `hingedSign_not_files`. Each case carries its reader's name and its disposition in the docstring. The rock fails as before, at `IsAccount`.

**(3) The prose.** A vK paragraph in the header; §12a (7), "The outside round, 11-4," after (6); the §13 paragraph: vK adds none — it narrows `Files` by one clause toward the word, at an outside reader's compiled case, as vI narrowed `Partial`, and what the clause says is what "read off the state" meant all along.

What did not change: `Stake`, `Reading`, `Partial`, `Loving`, `Edge`, the region section, `Direction`, `ActOn`, and everything above §12a. The thermostat's `Files` proof gains its third conjunct and nothing else.

## 5. What the run settles, and what it does not

**Settles.** `Files` at vJ admitted a thing whose account and whose guidance sat in different parts of its state and never met, and such a thing does not think; the clause that closes it is one line, it is what the prose always said ("read off the state"), and with it the sign fails by its author's own theorem while every case that was meant to file still files. The method's claim from the fifth narrowing is confirmed from outside: a reader told where the author stopped asking found the gap there, with a compiled case, inside a day. And the second declared weak point of the 11-3 note is now closed as declared, not by argument.

**Does not settle, and does not claim.** The first declared weak point, aboutness. `about` is still a field; the hot rock files at vK, compiled; and the clause that would separate the thermostat from the rock is not in the file. The separation CT has to hand is the holder's own direction, §12a (4): the thermostat's misfit — strip says cold, room warm — runs against what the thermostat moves toward (the set temperature), and the rock has no direction for its misfit to run against. Folding that into the filing line is the next run, at John's word, and until it is run the honest sentence is: `Files` is too wide at the first declared point, by a compiled case, and the fix is proposed and unbuilt. Nor does this run settle the wording question — whether CT says "thinks" of the floor; that is John's. And Omni has not reported; the count at the declared points is "one narrowing and no counterexample at the first point, by two readers," never "the definition is correct."

## 6. What this run says about the method

Seventh run; sixth narrowing; the first from outside. The five earlier narrowings were each found by the prover disagreeing with a proof the author tried to write. This one was found by a reader who was handed the definition, the two worked cases, the two declared weak points, and nothing else — not the prose, not the purpose — and asked to build a counterexample the machine could check. The blind hand-off did two things the in-house runs could not. It tested the definition against the ordinary word without the author's reading of the word in the room; and it turned the declared weak points from a confession into a target. The brief's own wording was also tested, and failed in one place: it said "thinks iff files" where CT says "files is the floor of thinking," and a reader found the gap between those two sentences with a vending machine. That is the right kind of failure for a brief to have, and it is recorded against the brief, not the file.

The rule the round adds, for the next one: a brief states CT's claim at the level CT states it — the floor, not the word — or it will draw counterexamples to its own sentence instead of to the definition.
