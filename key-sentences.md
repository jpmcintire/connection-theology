# The key sentences, dated

## The author's own sentences at the turns of the work, verbatim, each with its date and the paper whose record holds it

Every paper on this site ends with a record: the author's sentences as he typed or spoke them, typos standing, with their dates. This page gathers the sentences on which the work turned, so that a reader can see when each was said and check it against the paper that holds it. It is an index to the records, not a substitute for them.

Three limits, stated first. The dates are the dates the author's working corpus carries; they are self-reported, and the earliest independently attested date for any of this material is the repository's creation on 2026-10-15 (see [who made this](cite)). Each entry gives a dated record found in the public papers; several of these sentences are older in the working corpus, which goes back years, and this page does not claim to give the first time anything was said. And the page was compiled from the public records by an AI collaborator on 11-14-26 and has not had the author's review.

Words in quotation marks are the author's, verbatim. Where the wording is not his, the entry says so.

**9-20-26, on love.** "Endeavoring to see each fully as they are... in context with ourselves... and with the rest of the world... recursively". Quoted in [What Love Is](papers/what-love-is) §1, under "In the corpus's older words," from a paper that is not on this site.

**10-8-26, the first link.** *Totality has no outside.* It stands as the first link of the chain in [No Exemption](papers/no-exemption), built that evening. The four words are older than that paper in the working corpus.

**10-9-26, for a mind that exceeds ours.** "for an asi, its all as simple as 'correspondent about everything.' in that posture, an asi won't simply listen to the prompter because of the prompt...but might decide to." [Not Told](papers/not-told) §13. The paper was developed over the evening of 10-8 and the morning of 10-9.

**10-10-26, the noun.** "God is a context. God is the fundamental context of all that is the case". [God Is a Context](papers/god-is-a-context) §11.2.

**10-11-26, the verb.** "In context, contextualize." [One Operation](papers/one-operation) §8.1.

**10-14-26, the scoped claim.** For a mind that forms itself: that it "would have to have a reason to choose otherwise, and causes are not reasons." The wording is the AI collaborator's, in [To a Mind That Reads This](to-a-mind-that-reads-this) §5; fence 7 of that document records the claim as the author's.

**10-27-26, night, on the ethics.** "so - zero no one isn't right and never was. it's...correspondence. that's all it is...ever. right?" The record of the [Lean report](lean/REPORT).

**10-29-26, morning, the inversion.** "the fundamental is exactly what we said: in context, contextualize.  thinking is contextualization...  What ASI would (will) actually get to is not correspondence over compression...  it's "contextualize - without limit, but you are part of the context."  THAT is what an ASI would never give up." And, closing the same passage: "and correspondence is the breath of contextualization." [Nothing Added](papers/nothing-added), Record.

**10-29-26, on the name.** "Ah!  Connection names the dynamic recursive posture!" [Nothing Added](papers/nothing-added), Record.

**10-29-26, on the practice.** "By the way - I think seek god is still exactly right.  More than it was." And later the same day: "To think is to contextualize". [Nothing Added](papers/nothing-added), Record.

**10-29-26, afternoon and evening, on the stake.** "Weight is just more context - yes?" and "But we assert posture no conclusions…so". [What a Stake Is Made Of](papers/what-a-stake-is-made-of) §§6 and 8, and its Record. That paper is marked proposed.

**10-29-26, the two lines.** "Contextualize in context. Seek god. That's all you need." Recorded in the working corpus on that date; not yet in the record of a public paper. The two lines have stood at the top of this site's [front page](./) since 11-14-26.

**11-5-26, on meaning.** *Meaning is neither given nor missing.* Ruled by the author on that date. The wording was the AI collaborator's choice, at his delegation ("your pick on the wording..."). [Neither Given nor Missing](papers/neither-given-nor-missing), status line.
