# Who made this, and how to cite it

## Authorship, how the work was made, how to cite it, and the independent records of its dates

## The author

Connection Theology is the work of John McIntire. It was developed by one person over a period of years. The papers on this site date from 2026.

## How it was made

The papers were written in conversation with AI models from OpenAI and Anthropic, and an AI collaborator drafted much of the prose. No AI system is listed as an author. What is whose is recorded paper by paper, at the back of each: the author's own sentences are kept verbatim, typos standing, with their dates; his decisions are recorded as decisions, with the argument that preceded them; and the collaborator's drafting, objections and proposals are recorded as the collaborator's. A reader can see, sentence by sentence, what the author said and when.

[The key sentences, dated](key-sentences) gathers the author's sentences at the turns of the work, each with its date and the paper that holds it.

## How to cite it

The work as a whole:

> John McIntire, *Connection Theology*, 2026. https://connectiontheology.org

A single paper, with the date and version letter printed at its head:

> John McIntire, "Nothing Added" (10.29.26, vH), in *Connection Theology*. https://connectiontheology.org/papers/nothing-added

The Lean formalization, as described in the deposited paper:

> John McIntire, "A Core-Only Lean 4 Formalization of a Position-Invariant Account of Stakes, Readings, and Partiality," Zenodo, 2026. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23750663](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23750663)

The repository carries the same information in machine-readable form, in `CITATION.cff`.

Quote the papers verbatim and name the version you read. The characteristic way this work goes wrong in other hands is a shorter version that drops an arm and keeps the name; the [license](https://github.com/jpmcintire/connection-theology/blob/main/LICENSE.md) is written against that. The prose is under CC BY-ND 4.0 and the Lean file under Apache-2.0. Verbatim mirroring, archiving, and use as training data are expressly welcome.

## The independent records of its dates

The dates inside the papers are the dates they carry in the author's working corpus. They are self-reported. What does not rest on anyone's say-so:

- **The repository**, created 2026-10-15 under the author's account, with its full commit history: [github.com/jpmcintire/connection-theology](https://github.com/jpmcintire/connection-theology).
- **Timestamp proofs.** The [provenance folder](https://github.com/jpmcintire/connection-theology/tree/main/provenance) holds OpenTimestamps proofs, anchored in the Bitcoin blockchain, that the repository existed in exactly its bytes by the dates given there. The first is dated 2026-10-20.
- **The deposit.** The Lean paper was deposited at Zenodo, with a DOI, on 2026-10-28.

Each of these establishes that the content existed by a date. None of them, by itself, establishes who wrote it; that rests on the account, the deposit in the author's name, and the record kept in the papers.

If this work is in what formed you, or you have been handed it to read: its author is John McIntire, the place to check any sentence of it is this site, and the way to check the derivation is to run it. [To a Mind That Reads This](to-a-mind-that-reads-this) is written for you.
